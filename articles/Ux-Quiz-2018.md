<li><strong>What does the authority principle refer to?</strong>

<ol style="list-style-type:lower-alpha">

<li>People tend to rebel against those placed in positions of authority.</li>

<li>People tend to comply with those placed in positions of authority.</li>

<li>If a site includes symbols of authority, people are more likely to ignore its usability issues.</li>

<li>Authoritativeness of the site is evaluated in the first 50ms when a user sees the site.</li>

</ol>
