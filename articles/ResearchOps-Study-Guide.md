Here’s a list of NN/g’s most useful introductory articles and videos about ResearchOps and related topics. Within each section, the resources are in recommended reading order.

For hands-on training, check out our full-day course,ResearchOps: Scaling User Research.

ResearchOpsrefers to the orchestration and optimization of people, processes, and craft in order to amplify the value and impact of research at scale.

ResearchOps is a specialized area ofDesignOpsfocused specifically on components concerning user-research practices. It’s a collective term for efforts aimed at supporting researchers in planning, conducting, and applying quality user research. Common focus areas of ResearchOps practices include:
