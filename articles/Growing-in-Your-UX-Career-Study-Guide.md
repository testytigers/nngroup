UX practitioners come from a variety of backgrounds and bring numerous specialties, yet there are specific skills and responsibilities deemed crucialin the industry today. Here’s a compilation of NN/g’s most useful articles and videos about growing your UX career. We also share a free report based on research studies carried out with722 UX professionalsworldwide. Within each section, the resources are in recommended reading order.

This compilation of resources is geared toward existing UX practitioners and those considering a career transition into UX.  It’s meant to help you with career-related questions, such as which path to choose within UX or what to include in a research or design portfolio.

As people come to UX at very different stages of their lives and from different roles, the resources are divided by career levels (breaking-in,entry-level, andmid-level or senior), so you can jump to the content that applies to you. The first section below is dedicated to general UX-career advice and ways to present your work when applying for user-experience positions.
